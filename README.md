# Multimodal Variational Autoencoder for Seismic Images
> The repository has the implementation of a deep generative model based on a multimodal variational autoencoder (mVAE) for uncertainty quantification of seismic images. The mVAE model relies on a categorical latent-variable approach to represent the modes of the posterior probability distribution function. In addition, the model was designed to handle information of target regions of geological interest, allowing it to be guided by the specialist goals. To assess its performance, the mVAE model is compared with the traditional VAE using the variability index, pixel-wise amplitude distributions, and the Wasserstein distance, aiming to demonstrate that a multimodal formulation is essential for capturing and representing the full complexity of scientific data.

## Theoretical Background of the mVAE Implementation
The inference model (top), parameterized by the encoder $\mathcal{E}\_{\phi}(\mathbf{x})$, infers the discrete and continuous approximate posterior distributions. New samples are then generated by the generative model (bottom), parameterized by the decoder $\mathcal{D}\_{\theta}(\mathbf{z}, c)$, by sampling the discrete and continuous latent variables.
